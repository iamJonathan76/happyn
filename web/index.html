FAQ

How do I create my first event?
  1. In the app, tap +, then Event. Add a title, category, venue and dates.
  2. Set up free or paid tickets and choose how many spots to offer. Set up payouts before selling paid tickets.
  3. Choose public or private. Review the listing, publish it, and share the code if the event is private.
  4. Track sales from your event’s management screen. On the day, use the built-in scanner to check tickets.
Where is my ticket?

Open Tickets in the app and select your event. Show the QR from the app at the door.

How do I join a private event?

In Discover, tap “Have a code?” and enter the code from the organizer.

Can I host a free event?

Yes. Set your ticket price to $0 when configuring tickets in the app.
