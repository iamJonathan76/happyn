Everything between “what's on tonight?” and walking in

HAPPYN covers the whole path — discovery, payment, and entry — so you never have to leave the app to attend an event.

🔎

Discover what's near you

Browse events by category and city, save the ones you like, and keep an eye on what's coming up.

🎟️

Book in seconds

Free or paid, single or multiple tickets. Card payments are handled by Stripe — HAPPYN never stores your card details.

🔐

Tickets that can't be copied

Every ticket shows a signed QR code that refreshes regularly and can only be scanned once. A screenshot is worthless.

🔔

Stay in the loop

Purchase confirmations, event updates, cancellations — the things you actually need to know, in the app.

Run your event from your phone

Creating an event on HAPPYN takes a few minutes, and everything that follows happens in the same place.

  • Publish when you're ready. Draft it, publish it, unpublish it, cancel it — you stay in control of what's visible.
  • Set your own ticket tiers. Multiple price levels, limited quantities, and a cap on how many one person can buy.
  • Scan at the door. The built-in scanner admits each ticket exactly once, and only organizers of that event can use it.
  • Cancel cleanly. Cancelling an event marks the tickets accordingly and notifies the people who bought them.
